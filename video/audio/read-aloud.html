Recording it

One continuous take, straight through, to video/audio/vo-take.wav — 48 kHz, mono is fine. Don't stop and restart between lines; a single take is far easier to align to the picture than 29 fragments.

A quiet room matters more than an expensive microphone. Phone voice-memo held a hand's width away, off-axis so you aren't breathing straight into it, is enough. If you fluff a line, pause for three full seconds, then say the whole line again from the start — the clean retake is easy to find and splice.


What happens after

Hand the file over and scripts/build_voice_mix.py normalises it to the reference's measured loudness, peak-limits it, fits it to the 3:47 timeline and muxes it in. Only a re-encode is needed — the frames are already on disk, so the film is not re-rendered and nothing in the edit moves. The captions come from src/vo.ts and the beat boundaries from src/Main.tsx; neither is touched by audio, so your take cannot knock them out of sync.
