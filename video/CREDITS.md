# Credits — Arthaton submission video

Arthaton's rules require everything used to be credited. This lists every asset in
`video/out/arthaton-submission.mp4` and its licence.

## Audio

**There is no third-party audio in this video.** That is deliberate: rather than source a
royalty-free track and carry a licence obligation, the bed was synthesised to match the
reference's measured profile directly.

| Asset | What it is | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `public/audio/mix.wav` | The final mix — bed + scratch voice, ducked, loudness-normalised | Original work, generated by `scripts/build_audio.py` in this repo |
| `public/audio/bed.wav` | Music bed: a pulsed bass figure at **119.7 BPM**, synthesised in numpy/scipy from sine and filtered-noise primitives | Original work. No sample, loop or library was used |
| `public/audio/vo.wav` | **Scratch** voice-over, Windows SAPI (`System.Speech.Synthesis`) | Microsoft Windows component, local synthesis, no service call. **Placeholder — to be replaced by the author's own recording** |

The bed's tempo, RMS level and band balance are matched to measurements of the reference in
`analysis/audio_profile.json`. See `STYLE_SPEC.md` §6.2 for the measured targets and
`public/audio/audio_build.json` for what was achieved.

> **The voice is currently synthetic and is a placeholder.** It must be replaced with the
> author's own recording before submission — see `HUMAN_TASKS.md` task 1 and
> `audio/VO_SCRIPT.md`. The video should not be submitted implying the synthetic voice is
> the author's.

### On the Remotion licence

Remotion is free for individuals and small teams but **requires a paid company licence**
above a threshold. This video is a solo entry by an individual for a student hackathon and
falls inside the free terms. If any of this were ever used commercially, a licence would
have to be bought first. Recorded in `HUMAN_TASKS.md` so it is not forgotten.

## Fonts

No font files are bundled. The render uses system families (`Inter` where present,
otherwise Helvetica/Arial; Georgia for the serif-italic statement cards; Consolas/SF Mono
for the monospace strips). `STYLE_SPEC.md` §5 records that the reference's typeface could
only be **inferred** from glyph shape, never measured, so no claim is made that these match
it.

## The reference video

`WhatsApp Video 2026-11-17 at 7.12.51 PM.mp4` — the author's own prior submission for a
PayPal hackathon (project *Worth*). Used only as a **style template**: it was measured, and
the measurements informed this video's palette, cut rhythm, type scale and audio profile.
**No frame, asset or audio from it appears in this video.** It is the author's own work.

## Data

Every figure on screen is read at render time from this repository's committed artifacts —
`ml/artifacts/metrics.json`, `magnitude_metrics.json`, `confidence_tiers.json`,
`latest_signals.json`, and `ml/experiments/results/*.json` — through `src/data.ts`.
`src/honesty.test.ts` fails the build if a metric-shaped literal appears in any scene.

The one exception is the eight illustrative cards in beat 2.1, which are **invented** to
depict the genre of tool this project argues against. They are quarantined in
`src/illustrative.ts`, rendered greyed, and labelled *"Illustrative · invented figures ·
depicts the genre, not any real product"* on screen for the whole beat.

Underlying market data: Yahoo Finance via `yfinance` (Apache 2.0), as credited in the
repository README.
